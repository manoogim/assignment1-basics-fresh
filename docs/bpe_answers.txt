Both runs (4 and 12 workers) show that training time is dominated by pretokenization (400 out of 424) 
and pretokenization is dominated by regex(383 out of 400)
OpenWebText contest is much more diverse than stories writen in english language for children using english alphabet. 
Here we have multiple languages, multiple non-ascii scripts, legal and technical and scientific documents, scripts in multiple programming languagesetc. 
So there will be less frequency for the most frequent words, 
which for BPE tokenizer means it will have shorter tokens in dict and will need more tokens to represent words.

Imortant discovery:
Large dataset of more than 2GB surfaced shortcommings of my memory management: number of workers equals number of chunks, which means 
that entire dataset is present in memory at the same time. 
Result is constant page faults (Task manager shows 99% memory and 99% disk.)
Solution: (a) allow many small chunks and 2 or 4 parallel workers and (b) Free chunk of raw bytes after decoding them in `pretokenize_chunk`
Result is 26 minutes of training (1595 sec), with 1195 sec pretokenization and 400 sec merge loop.

What can we say about throughput (bytes/sec) for pretokenization step?
Total size of owt is 6GB, and it was processed in 1195 seconnds so answer is 5MB per second.

How long would it take to tokenize Pile dataset( 825GB )?
Answer = 825,000,000,000 / 5000000 = 165,000 sec or 46 hours
